HOW IT STARTED

Our Story

Noor Fashion Boutique was established in 2022 in Lahore, Pakistan, with a simple idea: help customers turn their clothing ideas into outfits that reflect their personal style.

It began as a small boutique concept focused on custom stitching, everyday clothing and special-occasion outfits. Our goal is to make clothing choices easier through friendly service, thoughtful designs and careful finishing.

Note: This business history is fictional and prepared for the student assignment.

Our Mission

To help customers express their individual style through thoughtful designs, quality stitching and reliable customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local boutique known for creativity, comfort and customer satisfaction.
